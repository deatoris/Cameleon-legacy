5 & 7 years !

11 Jan, 2014 Caméléon Image Processing Population Olivier Cugnon de Sévricourt

Today ‘s the Caméléon birthday of its five-year and Population has started for 7 years! A long road!

A little history

Caméléon represents 3 major versions where each time we started all over again, 150 000 lines of code, 5 & 7 years of hard work for Caméléon and Population respectively, 50 GB of data, thousands of sketches & a really big svn repository.

We are working hard on the next step, so keep watching us
